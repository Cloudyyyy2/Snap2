## Problem Statement
Real phone photos of handwritten lists scanned through Snap2's local Python OCR sidecar (`python_ocr/`, port 8001) emit multi-word Wikipedia, date, and citation hallucinations instead of the actual handwritten items. In baseline diagnostic `run-20261122-033608-038-auto` (17 lines, Quality 0.66) on a 14-item ruled spiral-notebook grocery list (`media_1795289813276_c93e39f5.jpg`), `eggs` became `"after the success"`, `Salmon` became `"Cecrezo's first appearance in the Spanish Spanish"`, `broccoli` became `"May 20 2021 May 20 2021"`, and noise/clipped margin artifacts produced `"1953 Southern State"`, `"2 References"`, `"- thu"`, and `"I : - :"`.

Diagnostic probes across `python_ocr/` and `public/models/` establish three compounding root causes:
1. **Architecture inversion in `python_ocr/recognizer.py`:** `recognize_image_bytes` runs `OfflineTrOcrEngine` (`trocr-base-handwritten` q8 ONNX) *first* on every line crop (`recognizer.py:302-324`). On isolated messy single-word handwriting crops, ViT visual features are sparse, so TrOCR's autoregressive RoBERTa decoder falls back to IAM/Wikipedia language priors at low confidence (`0.05-0.55`). Meanwhile `ParseqOcrEngine` (`public/models/parseq/model.onnx`, a fixed-vocab `[3,32,128]` permutation recognizer structurally incapable of multi-word prose confabulation) already reads almost all 14 items at `0.74-0.99` confidence when given clean un-eroded grayscale (`no_rules_gray`).
2. **Broken plausibility and hallucination gates in `python_ocr/postprocess.py`:** `score_text_plausibility()` (`postprocess.py:160-170`) awards `0.35 * (lex_hits / tokens) + 0.20 * alpha_ratio = 0.55` regardless of confidence, so `"after the success"` at `0.258` conf scores `0.666 >= 0.65` and bypasses PARSeq completely. `detect_and_clean_hallucination()` (`postprocess.py:121-157`) also misses 2-4 word n-gram loops (`"May 20 2021 May 20 2021"`), month+year date loops, Wikipedia phrases (`"first appearance in"`, `"References"`), and short symbol-junk lines (`"I : - :"`).
3. **Four compounding preprocessing defects in `python_ocr/preprocess.py`:** (a) `strip_spiral_and_corner_artifacts()` (`preprocess.py:243-287`) deletes `x <= 3` edge coils before periodicity clustering, dropping consistent spacing votes from `10` to `9` (threshold `>= 10`) and leaving top-half spiral coils intact; (b) clipped right-edge second-column fragments (`"who..."`, `"in..."`) survive and trigger `split_wide_line_box()` (`preprocess.py:346-374`) to emit `"- thu"` and slice `"milk"` mid-word; (c) `remove_ruled_lines()` (`preprocess.py:207-217`) removes `0` pixels on slightly tilted/broken notebook rules (`108x1` kernel too strict), leaving `7x1` rule dashes that stretch `x1` across blank paper; (d) `contrast_gray` is never masked by removed artifacts and loses thin cursive strokes to `medianBlur(3)` + `p_high=78.0`, degrading `spinach` to `spinod`.

## Demand Evidence
- **Concrete failure on live production sidecar:** Probing live `POST loopback port 8001 `/ocr`` on `media_1795289813276_c93e39f5.jpg` reproduces `ocr-exports/run-20261122-033608-038-auto/extracted_text.txt` identically: 17 regions instead of 14 items, 3 full prose/date hallucinations, 2 clipped/split junk lines, and corrupted prefixes (`"L bread"`, `"1 Panan chocolat"`, `"1 Cas Parius"`, `": hotel mince"`).
- **Direct user impact on Snap2 core job:** Snap2 routes both `POST /api/local-ocr` (`server.ts:287-370`) and `/api/extract-reminder` offline fallback (`server.ts:631-641`) through `python_ocr/` on port 8001 into `parseReminderLocally`. When `eggs` turns into `"after the success"` and `broccoli` turns into `"May 20 2021 May 20 2021"`, Snap2 creates nonsense reminders with false dates instead of a usable grocery checklist.
- **Passing unit suite masks production failure:** `test_ocr.py` (4/4) and `test_benchmark_parity.py` (40/40) pass at HEAD `c31d689` only because their E2E fixtures use synthetic PIL fonts (`arial.ttf`, `segoepr.ttf`) on clean white canvases (`test_benchmark_parity.py:62-81, 223-262`) rather than real spiral-bound ruled notebook scans.

## Status Quo
- **Working reference in Node/TS (`server/handwritingRecognizer.ts`):** Snap2's older Node handwriting pipeline already solved these failure modes by preserving crisp `parseqGray` (`handwritingRecognizer.ts:482`), running **PARSeq-first** (`handwritingRecognizer.ts:834-965`) with adjacent-span merge disambiguation, stripping top/bottom line bleeds (`stripLineEdgeBleeds` in `src/lib/ocr/handwriting/lines.ts:1217-1326`), penalizing low-confidence plausibility (`src/lib/ocr/plausibility.ts:146-147`), and blocking n-gram/date/Wikipedia loops (`src/lib/ocr/postprocess.ts:18-117`).
- **Python sidecar regression (`python_ocr/`):** Built later (`c31d689`), `python_ocr/` inverted the recognition order to TrOCR-first and omitted `parseqGray`, `stripLineEdgeBleeds`, adjacent-span merge, and the stricter plausibility/hallucination checks. Because `server.ts:65-133` auto-spawns and prefers the port-8001 Python sidecar whenever healthy, live Snap2 users hit the broken Python path instead of the working TS design.

## Premises
1. **Architectural inversion, not KV-cache corruption, causes the hallucinations.** Verified by probe: `OfflineTrOcrEngine` cached vs uncached outputs match identically (`0.852` on `"busy milk tomorrow"`), while all real single-word crops trigger RoBERTa LM priors at `0.05-0.55` conf across padding/beam settings. *(Auto-agreed)*
2. **PARSeq + clean preprocessing already handles handwritten list items offline.** `ParseqOcrEngine` reads real grocery words at `0.74-0.99` conf on `no_rules_gray` once spiral coils, clipped right-column text, rule dashes, and vertical descender bleeds are stripped. *(Auto-agreed)*
3. **Reuse Snap2's proven TS patterns inside `python_ocr/` rather than inventing new heuristics.** Porting `parseqGray`, `stripLineEdgeBleeds`, adjacent-span merge, n-gram/date hallucination checks, and low-confidence plausibility penalties keeps TS/Python parity (`test_benchmark_parity.py`) and minimizes risk. *(Auto-agreed)*
4. **All fixes belong inside `python_ocr/` without modifying `server.ts` or TS lexicon sync.** Adding `"tomatoes"` to `GROCERY_CORE_WORDS` inside `python_ocr/postprocess.py` and scoping fixes to `python_ocr/` satisfies R2 cleanly. *(Auto-agreed)*

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: Postprocessing + Plausibility Threshold Patch (Minimal Viable)
- **Summary:** Keep `preprocess.py` line segmentation and TrOCR-first order mostly as-is; expand `detect_and_clean_hallucination()` with n-gram/date/Wikipedia filters, lower plausibility for `< 0.55` confidence so PARSeq runs more often, and add grocery aliases to `postprocess.py`.
- **Effort:** `S` (human: ~0.5 day / CC: ~15 min) | **Risk:** `Med-High`
- **Pros:**
  - Smallest diff touching only `recognizer.py` (few lines) and `postprocess.py`.
  - Lowest risk of altering `01_original.png` .. `05_adaptive_binary.png` stage visuals or breaking synthetic line counts.
- **Cons:**
  - Leaves all 4 `preprocess.py` defects intact: `regionsCount` stays 17 instead of 14 (`I : - :`, `- thu`, split `milk`), spiral coils (`"L"`, `"1"`, `":"`) remain inside word crops, `7x1` rule dashes keep stretching `broccoli` to `x1=624`, and `medianBlur` `contrast_gray` still erodes `spinach` into `spinod`.
  - Forces brittle regex/alias hacks to recover corrupted strings like `"1 Cas Parius"`, `": hotel mince"`, and `"- a m"` / `"IK - :"`.
- **Reuses:** Existing `detect_and_clean_hallucination()` and `MISREAD_ALIASES`.
- *Rejected:* Treats symptoms after `preprocess.py` has already corrupted the crops and split `"milk"` in half.

### Approach B: End-to-End Parity Pipeline (Clean Segmentation + PARSeq-First Crisp Input + Hardened TrOCR Gate) *(Selected — Ideal Architecture)*
- **Summary:** Fix the 4 root-cause defects in `preprocess.py` (pre-edge-delete spiral spine clustering, clipped right-column removal, tilted/broken rule suppression, mask propagation to `contrast_gray` and `no_rules_gray`, wide-gap-only `split_wide_line_box`, outlier-trimmed `x0/x1`, and `strip_line_edge_bleeds`), flip `recognizer.py` handwriting to **PARSeq-first on crisp `no_rules_gray`** with adjacent-span merge + full-line sanity fallback + speck filtering while hardening `OfflineTrOcrEngine` behind strict confidence/hallucination gates, and port Snap2 TS hallucination/plausibility/lexicon parity fixes into `postprocess.py`.
- **Effort:** `M` (human: ~2 days / CC: ~45 min) | **Risk:** `Low`
- **Pros:**
  - Eliminates hallucinations at all three pipeline stages: clean 14-line crops, non-prose primary recognizer (`ParseqOcrEngine`), and strict TrOCR confidence/n-gram/date gates.
  - Achieves architectural parity with Snap2's verified `server/handwritingRecognizer.ts` and `src/lib/ocr/` modules.
  - Preserves fast CPU latency (`ParseqOcrEngine` `[3,32,128]` runs far faster than autoregressive TrOCR ONNX on every line) and maintains `>= 90%` accuracy on synthetic printed/cursive E2E fixtures.
- **Cons:**
  - Touches all three core modules (`preprocess.py`, `recognizer.py`, `postprocess.py`) plus `python_ocr/tests/`.
  - Requires careful geometric thresholds so tilted rule removal never erases horizontal `t`/`f` crossbars and right-margin clipping never drops genuine end-of-line words like `"tomatoes"` or `"tomorrow"`.
- **Reuses:** `server/handwritingRecognizer.ts:482,834-965` (`parseqGray`, PARSeq-first, adjacent-span merge), `src/lib/ocr/handwriting/lines.ts:1217-1326` (`stripLineEdgeBleeds`), `src/lib/ocr/postprocess.ts:18-117`, and `src/lib/ocr/plausibility.ts:91-156`.

### Approach C: Unconditional Dual-Engine Consensus Reranker (Creative / Lateral)
- **Summary:** Run both PARSeq (word + full-line) and TrOCR (aspect-padded + beam=2) unconditionally on every detected line, align outputs via edit distance / character-level consensus, and discard lines with high inter-engine divergence or low consensus score.
- **Effort:** `M-L` (human: ~3-4 days / CC: ~75 min) | **Risk:** `Med`
- **Pros:**
  - Symmetric handling across printed and handwritten styles without needing a hard style classifier boundary.
  - Could catch rare out-of-vocabulary proper nouns where TrOCR has high visual confidence (`>= 0.85`) and PARSeq has a single-letter error.
- **Cons:**
  - Multiplies CPU inference latency per scan by running quantized TrOCR encoder+decoder ONNX on all 14+ lines even when PARSeq already achieves `0.95+` confidence.
  - Still fails on `- thu`, split `milk`, and `spinod` unless `preprocess.py` is also fixed, and risks TrOCR prose hallucinations winning vocabulary tie-breakers if consensus scoring is miscalibrated.
- **Reuses:** `OfflineTrOcrEngine`, `ParseqOcrEngine`, `score_text_plausibility`.
- *Rejected:* Adds 5-10x latency overhead without removing the preprocessing root causes; Approach B already invokes TrOCR selectively when PARSeq is empty/low-confidence.

## Recommended Approach
**Selected: Approach B (End-to-End Parity Pipeline)** *(Auto-approved per spawned subagent policy).*

### 1. Preprocessing & Segmentation (`python_ocr/preprocess.py`)
- **Crisp grayscale preservation (`PreprocessResult`):** Store `no_rules_gray` (or upscaled `parseq_gray` aligned to `contrast_gray` / `binary` coordinates) on `PreprocessResult`, and scale `binarize_adaptive` Sauvola `window` proportionally with `dpi_scale` (e.g., `int(round(25 * dpi_scale)) | 1`) while raising `enhance_contrast` `p_high` from `78.0` to `88.0-90.0` so thin strokes (`spinach`) are preserved.
- **Tilted/broken ruled-line suppression (`remove_ruled_lines`):** Supplement the strict `max(35, w // 4) x 1` morphological line open with multi-angle / broken-rule suppression (e.g., small ±2° affine-sheared horizontal openings or connected-component filtering of very thin, wide horizontal strokes `ch <= 3`, `cw / ch >= 8`) gated so letter crossbars (`t`, `f`) attached to tall vertical stems are untouched.
- **Spiral & right-column artifact stripping (`strip_spiral_and_corner_artifacts`) + mask propagation:**
  - Collect `left_cands` across the full left margin (`x + cw <= w * 0.22` or `cx <= w * 0.18`, `area >= 40`) *before* deleting `x <= 3` edge-touching coils, cluster along a linear/slanted spine with periodic vertical spacing, strip the spiral column plus secondary margin specks (`x + cw <= max_spiral_right + 4`), and *then* remove border-touching edge artifacts.
  - Strip isolated right-edge clipped second-column fragments (`x >= w * 0.85` separated by a large horizontal whitespace gap `>= max(32, int(w * 0.12))` and touching/near `x + cw >= w - 6` with narrow cluster width `< w * 0.14`), preserving legitimate end-of-line words (`tomatoes`, `tomorrow`).
  - Paint `removed_mask = (raw_bin == 0) & (binary == 255)` (dilated 1-2px) to `255` on both `contrast_gray` and `no_rules_gray` inside `run_preprocessing()` so removed artifacts never leak into `06_line_XX.png` or recognizer crops.
- **Clean line segmentation & bleed stripping (`segment_lines`, `split_wide_line_box`, `strip_line_edge_bleeds`):**
  - Drop low-ink noise peaks (`ink_pixels < max(40, int(bh * 0.9))` or no component with `area >= 18` and `ch >= 6`), eliminating Line 1 (`"I : - :"`).
  - Trim isolated horizontal speck outliers (`ch <= 3`, `<= 25` ink pixels separated by `> max(48, bh * 2.2)`) when computing `x0, x1`, stopping `7x1` rule dashes from stretching `broccoli` to `x1=624`.
  - Restrict `split_wide_line_box()` to genuine wide inter-column whitespace gaps (`>= max(24, int(bh * 0.8))` near-zero columns) so `"milk"` is never sliced in half.
  - Port `stripLineEdgeBleeds` from `src/lib/ocr/handwriting/lines.ts:1217-1326` to strip vertical ascender/descender bleeds invading from adjacent lines (`p` in `pan au chocolat` / `spinach` invading `lunch for tomorrow` / `turkey mince`), and pad line/word crops with clean white (`255`) margins.

### 2. Recognition Routing & TrOCR Hardening (`python_ocr/recognizer.py`)
- **PARSeq-first for `detected_style == "handwriting"`:**
  - Segment words from `prep.binary` and crop from crisp un-eroded `prep.no_rules_gray` (with `strip_line_edge_bleeds` mask applied and white padding).
  - Drop tiny punctuation-only or narrow `< 16px` `< 0.50`-conf speck crops before joining.
  - Implement adjacent-span merge disambiguation (`server/handwritingRecognizer.ts:870-918`) so faintly broken words (`as` + `paragus`, `mi` + `lk`) merge and re-evaluate cleanly.
  - Add full-line PARSeq sanity fallback for single-word-aspect lines (`aspect <= 5.5`) when word-splitting fragments a single word.
  - Keep `OfflineTrOcrEngine` primary for `detected_style == "printed"` (and wide multi-word synthetic lines where word segmentation yields an ultra-wide crop or PARSeq confidence/plausibility is low and TrOCR passes gates).
- **Harden `OfflineTrOcrEngine` (`recognizer.py:40-146`):**
  - Replace raw `cv2.resize(crop_gray, (384, 384))` in `_prepare_pixel_values()` with aspect-preserving resize + centered `255` white padding onto `384x384` (preventing character warping on short words).
  - Add constrained decoding: `no_repeat_ngram_size=3` (masking repeating 3-gram logits during ONNX step loop), repetition penalty `1.1`, and `max_new_tokens` capped tighter (`32`, or `16` on narrow short-word crops `w/h < 3.5`).
  - Gate TrOCR acceptance strictly: reject TrOCR candidates when `trocr_conf < 0.60`, when `detect_and_clean_hallucination()` flags them, or when a narrow single-word crop (`w/h < 3.5`) emits `>= 3` space-separated words. On `handwriting`, only fall back to TrOCR if PARSeq is empty or below plausibility/confidence threshold (`< 0.62`) AND TrOCR passes all gates and strictly beats PARSeq plausibility by margin.

### 3. Hallucination Detection, Plausibility & Lexicon Parity (`python_ocr/postprocess.py`)
- **Expand `detect_and_clean_hallucination()` (`postprocess.py:121-157`)** matching `src/lib/ocr/postprocess.ts:18-117` plus date/boilerplate guards:
  - Reject pure punctuation (`^[^A-Za-z0-9]+$`), single-letter runs (`^(?:[a-z]\s+){4,}[a-z]\b`), zero loops, and token diversity collapse (`words >= 10` and `unique_ratio < 0.42`).
  - Reject 2-, 3-, and 4-word n-gram loops (including `>= 2` repeats for 2-3 word date/phrase loops like `"May 20 2021 May 20 2021"` and consecutive duplicated long words like `"Spanish Spanish"` when paired with boilerplate or low confidence).
  - Reject Wikipedia/citation/date prose hallucinations (`"first appearance in"`, `"References"`, `"Categories"`, `"century"`, `"Southern State"`, `"age the age of birth"`, Wikipedia sidebar terms, and month+year patterns with repeated years or low conf `< 0.70`).
  - Reject residual symbol-junk fragments (`"I : - :"`, `"IK - :"`, `"- a m"`, `"- thu"`).
- **Fix `score_text_plausibility()` (`postprocess.py:160-170`):**
  - Apply heavy low-confidence penalty / cap (e.g., matching `src/lib/ocr/plausibility.ts:146-147` plus capping below `0.45` when `avg_conf < 0.50`) so `0.26`-confidence `"after the success"` can never score `>= 0.65` and block PARSeq. Penalize isolated punctuation/single-char noise tokens.
- **Close the 5 targeted handwriting/grocery parity gaps (`postprocess.py`):**
  - Allow `VISUAL_CONFUSION_PAIRS` in `starts_match` (`postprocess.py:378-395`) when `len(lower) >= 5` and `best_w <= 1.5` (enabling `('j','s')` for `Jolnun`/`Jalman` -> `Salmon`).
  - Expand `build_ligature_variants()` (`^vi` -> `bro` plus aliases `vioulli`, `viralli`, `vjoulli` -> `broccoli` in `MISREAD_ALIASES`, and `t[io]rm{1,2}[wv]` / `timmav` -> `tomorrow`).
  - Expand `repair_conservative_phrases()` for `Pain au chocolat` (`pa[nm]a[nu]\s+ch[oa]{1,2}[cr]?o?lat`), `"Sundried <torthel|parshel|...>"` -> `"Sundried tomatoes"`, and `"lunch (?:fir|fiy|fr) tomorrow"` -> `"lunch for tomorrow"` (preventing `"fir"` from snapping to weekday `"fri"`).
  - Add `"tomatoes"` to `GROCERY_CORE_WORDS` (`postprocess.py:75-84`).
  - Guard `"spinod"`/`"spinad"` -> `"spinach"` (avoiding false snap to `"spend"`).
